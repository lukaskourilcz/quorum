# FightAIQ doctrine

FightAIQ is a sourced fight-analysis desk for UFC and Oktagon. It keeps each
organization’s ratings separate. Decision D8 now permits guarded live analysis when
the bout and both fighter cards pass their evidence checks.

## What the desk does

- Builds public fighter files whose facts link back to dated sources.
- Keeps missing, provisional and disputed fields visible.
- Runs one deterministic, versioned probability model and shows its uncertainty.
- Compares fair probabilities with captured market prices after removing the margin.
- Records whether earlier probabilities were well calibrated.
- Produces ten concise fight notes only when the whole slate passes its data checks.

## What the desk never does

- Place a bet, sign into a bookmaker or publish a bookmaker or affiliate link.
- Call anything certain, risk-free, guaranteed or a route to income.
- Hide a losing record, disagreement, missing source or model change.
- Mix ratings between UFC and Oktagon.
- Let a narrative adjustment move a probability by more than three percentage points.
- Use a source whose access rules have not been cleared.

All gambling-adjacent material is for adults and entertainment only. Every slate says
that staking money carries an expected loss, and that nobody should chase a loss or use
money needed elsewhere.
