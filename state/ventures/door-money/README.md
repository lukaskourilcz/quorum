# Door Money state

This public directory stores review-only Door Money drafts, bounded derivative records and
owner-entered outcomes. Nothing here is published, scheduled or sent by the system. Every
external action remains the owner's decision and work.

The manuscript, full-text chunks and embeddings never belong in this repository. The private
store is the owner-created source repository identified by **BOOK-SOURCE-001** (working pointer:
`lukaskourilcz/rapovej-denik-source`), accessed only after countersignature with the repository-
scoped, contents-only `BOOK_SOURCE_TOKEN`. Local ingestion may instead read the ignored
`state/ventures/door-money/manuscript/` directory.

Committed excerpts are derivatives capped at 600 characters. Style-profile exemplars are capped
at 40 entries of 280 characters each. Until the private-source and ingestion approvals are
countersigned, the desk stays on fixture-backed dry paths and records that it held at $0.
