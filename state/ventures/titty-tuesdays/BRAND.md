# Titty Tuesdays

Status: pre-commerce

Category: women's crop tops, and only women's crop tops

## Motto

“We only sell sexy crop tops with the text TITTY TUESDAYS on it.”

The narrowness is the position: one garment, one joke, executed with care. Every
product keeps the exact words `TITTY TUESDAYS` on the garment. Until an owner-built
eshop connects through a future delivery contract, every product remains a concept
and nobody may claim it is available to buy.

## Codes

- Bold typography that holds up from across a room.
- Streetwear and skate adjacency without borrowed logos or scene cosplay.
- Self-aware humor. The name carries the provocation; the supporting copy stays clever.
- Product detail, fabric, labels, flat lays and construction before lifestyle theatre.

## Boundaries

- No explicit imagery or copy.
- No shock for its own sake.
- No fast-fashion volume or fake scarcity.
- No generated human figures or body-focused compositions.
- No audience under 18.
- No commerce, payment, inventory or availability claims in this stage.
- No live post while `SOCIAL_KILL_SWITCH` is true.
