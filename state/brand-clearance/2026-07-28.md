# BoardlessAI brand clearance — owner decision

Decided: 2026-07-28
Decider: Lukas Kouril (owner)
Status: **accepted collision risk under hobby / non-commercial mode**

## Decision

The owner has explicitly accepted the collision risk documented in
`2026-07-23.md` with:

- `Boardless, Inc.` (AI-operated company, `@boardless_inc`)
- `Boardless` remote developer jobs product (`boardless.dev`)
- Registered domain `boardless.ai`

The name `BoardlessAI` is retained as the working title for this personal
project.

## Scope of acceptance

This acceptance is bounded by the project's hobby / non-commercial mode
(see `state/BUSINESS.md` → Project mode). Specifically:

- No commercial launch, no revenue collection, no advertising.
- No domain purchase using this name.
- No handle registration on social platforms using this name.
- No trademark claim, no legal opposition to any of the colliding parties.
- Portfolio card at `lukaskouril.dev` may continue to use the name because it
  describes a personal project, not a competing product.

## Revisit triggers

This decision must be revisited before any of the following:

- Reclassifying the project as commercial in `state/BUSINESS.md`.
- Purchasing any domain, or registering any social/marketplace handle under
  this name.
- Publishing paid content or advertising under this name.
- Any pivot that would make the project more visible to the colliding
  parties.

At any of those points, professional legal clearance is required, or the
project must be renamed.

## Notes

This is not a legal opinion. It is the operator's informed acceptance of a
documented low-stakes risk while the project remains a private, unpaid
exercise.
