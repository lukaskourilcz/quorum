# BoardlessAI brand clearance revisit

Date: 2026-08-01

Status: collision risk remains open

## Finding

The move from hobby use to operating, pre-revenue mode triggers the revisit terms
in `2026-07-28.md`. BoardlessAI remains at documented collision risk with
Boardless, Inc. and `boardless.ai`. This record is an operating decision, not a
legal opinion or professional clearance.

## Mitigation

- Caught Up carries all public product identity.
- Any future sponsorship or revenue attaches to Caught Up.
- BoardlessAI remains the studio label for the governance system.
- The owner must rename or clear BoardlessAI before accepting the first paid
  sponsorship.

The final item is a hard gate. A meeting, delivery or publisher cannot waive it.
