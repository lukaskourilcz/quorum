## Domain and handle screen

- `boardless.ai` resolves and WHOIS reports a registration created
  2026-01-10. It must be treated as unavailable/third-party controlled.
- Verisign RDAP returned `404` for `boardlessai.com` at the time of the check,
  and CZ.NIC WHOIS returned no entry for `boardlessai.cz`. DNS/WHOIS status can
  change instantly and is not an authorization to register or use either name.
- Social search did not reliably establish availability of `@boardlessai`.
  Platform-native checks and owner approval are still required.

## Language and confusion review

- English: “boardless” naturally suggests operating without a board and is
  descriptive/suggestive for this concept.
- Czech: the intended idea reads as “firma bez představenstva/správní rady”.
  The English mark has no obvious offensive Czech meaning, but pronunciation
  and spelling are not inherently Czech.
- The exact-adjacent Japanese AI-operated company creates a material likelihood
  of source/concept confusion even if a concatenated spelling were technically
  registrable.

## Decision

Keep `BoardlessAI` only as a provisional working title inside this repository.
Do not buy a domain, register a mark, create handles, publish a launch claim or
represent clearance. Before public launch:

1. obtain professional trademark/company-name review in intended jurisdictions;
2. have the owner decide whether the semantic collision is acceptable;
3. recheck domains and platform handles in native registries;
4. record an explicit approval or rename decision in `state/INBOX.md`.
