## When the limit gets close

At 80%, the daily summary must warn the owner and show spend by project. At 100%, or
after three straight days that exhaust the daily pace, all further spend stops. The
system sends one immediate alert for the period and opens one approval item showing
the breakdown, the work already reduced and the saving from each further option.

Only the owner can raise or redistribute the limit. The system cannot borrow from next
month, silently move money between categories or keep spending while it waits.
