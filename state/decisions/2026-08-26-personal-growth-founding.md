## Information boundary

Protected Admin may resolve the workspace. Public portfolio lists, counts, calendars, meeting
feeds, facilities, project routes, sitemaps, generated project tables and KPI groupings must exclude
its identity and state. Public documentation may report one owner-only workspace without naming it
or exposing configuration, recommendations, manuscript metadata, analytics or results.

## Authority

Implementation and dry proof are authorized. The project itself may be registered as live, but
each paid or external capability has its own gate. This record grants no account creation, purchase,
credential, OAuth scope, analytics access, platform search, automatic queue or publishing authority.
The nested budget and company cap are governed by `budget-2026-08f`.

## Degradation and stops

The project must degrade deterministically from full recommendations to one primary recommendation,
then accepted-evidence reuse, then deterministic reminders and manual result entry. At the nested or
company limit it makes no paid call and records a budget-held result. Missing optional input remains
unavailable and never becomes a fabricated value.
