# Public money and quarterly KPI system

Date: 2026-08-02

Decider: Lukas Kouril, owner

Status: countersigned

Decision id: `D10`

## Owner direction

- Publish one English Money page with the company and project KPIs, earning
  methods, activation conditions, operating costs and recognized revenue.
- Run each operating quarter for 90 days. Q1 starts on 2026-08-03 unless the
  owner changes `config/kpis/2026-Q1.json` before the first evaluation.
- Treat the first 14 days of Q1 as a spin-up period for content and social pace
  calculations.
- Evaluate KPIs from saved records at $0. Missing Phase 3 measurements remain
  unavailable until the owner enables their lawful collection.
- Create a mandatory strategy reassessment when a project passes fewer than 70%
  of its KPIs or misses one critical KPI. The decision at stake is `continue /
  pivot / stop`.
- Send a company-level miss to the board so it reviews its own operating pattern.
- Prepare a complete earning proposal when an activation condition becomes
  ready. The system may move a method from `locked` to `ready` or `proposed`.
- Keep `active` under owner control. This decision does not authorize commerce,
  payments, accounts, affiliate programs, advertising or legal acts.

## Cost and revenue rules

- Use `state/budget/ledger.json` as the API cost record.
- Add owner-entered fixed subscriptions from `config/fixed-costs.json`.
- Publish amounts and cost categories only. Do not publish invoices,
  credentials or personal data.
- Keep recognized revenue at `$0.00` until the owner accepts a real revenue
  event.
- Count every monthly operating cost against the countersigned `$50` all-in
  limit and keep API spending within its `$42` share.

## Q1 target authority

The seed values live in `config/kpis/2026-Q1.json`. The owner can edit them at
any time. A runtime process cannot lower a target or rewrite a past evaluation.

FightAIQ tracks roster coverage, file completeness, forecast coverage,
evaluated events and calibration reports. Q1 does not target betting profit or
prediction accuracy.

## Activation boundaries

- Caught Up may prepare a sponsorship proposal after its saved reach condition
  passes.
- MMA Files additionally requires the owner to enable indexing. Bookmaker
  affiliate programs require a separate owner and legal decision.
- Titty Tuesdays may prepare a season-one commerce proposal after its campaign
  and follower conditions pass. The owner must build and approve the shop.
- FightAIQ has no earning method in Q1 or Q2. Review starts after 30 evaluated
  events with published calibration and a separate owner and legal decision.
- The Incubator remains a research function.
