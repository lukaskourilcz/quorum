# FightAIQ founding

Date: 2026-08-02

Decider: Lukas Kouril, owner

Status: pending owner countersignature

Decision id: `fightaiq-founding`

## Proposed decision

The owner adopts FightAIQ as Venture 003. Its first operating mode is data-only:
verified UFC, KSW and Oktagon fighter files, event cards, versioned model work and a
private owner review desk.

FightAIQ may explain probabilities and compare its model with captured market prices.
It may not place a bet, open a bookmaker, publish an affiliate link, claim certainty
or present gambling as income. Public material is 18+ entertainment content and must
show uncertainty, source gaps and the expected-loss warning.

Live analysis remains off until the owner reviews one complete event cycle from each
organization. A source with unclear or hostile terms stays disabled. Important fighter
fields need two agreeing sources before entering the model.

## Effect before countersignature

Contracts, adapters, the deterministic engine, dry meetings, public sourced-data pages
and private admin tools may ship. Scheduled live calls and public probabilities stay off.
