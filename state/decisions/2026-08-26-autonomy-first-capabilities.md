## Company priority

BoardlessAI applies this order when goals compete:

1. correctness, privacy and evidence;
2. reliable operation;
3. efficient execution;
4. narrow routine autonomy inside recorded authority;
5. audience growth through each venture's own approved channels;
6. monetization only after a new owner decision.

Monetization is informational planning only. A catalog entry cannot create work, an experiment,
an agenda, an offer, a price, outreach, commerce, affiliate placement, sponsor contact,
subscription, provider use or spend. No board, agent, venture, Admin control or scheduler may
convert readiness evidence into execution authority.

## Capability posture

Venture content and private data are denied by default. Every allowed cross-venture or service
relationship is directional, capability-specific, schema-bound and versioned. Shared topics,
tags, file proximity, similar shapes and access to generic infrastructure never create an edge.
A capability allowance is not publishing, provider, connection, account, budget or spend
authority; the stricter independent gate always wins.

The versioned map under `config/venture-capabilities.json` is the current machine-readable
boundary. Unknown nodes, capabilities, schemas and edges fail closed. Changes require a later
history-preserving decision and map version.

## Permanent isolation

- BOOKSOFHISTORY and Tehdejší svět exchange no candidates, research, dossiers, claims, drafts,
  translations, agendas, performance priors, content or campaigns in either direction.
- Personal Growth has no automatic portfolio reader, scanner, feed, nomination path, Kvórum
  bridge or Social Distribution campaign input. Its 85/15 rule applies only to references the
  owner manually supplies or selects.
- Kvórum emits no political recommendations, claims, evidence, content or campaign payload to
  another venture, profile, Personal Growth, the company umbrella or Social Distribution.
- FightAIQ has no premium-picks, betting-affiliate, paid-feed, subscription, sponsor or
  data-licensing execution capability.
- GoVIRAL may provide bounded intelligence and evidence only. It cannot provide final copy,
  captions, articles, carousel copy, calls to action, campaign packages or publishing actions.
- Door Money's only content/service relationships are accepted GoVIRAL intelligence, bounded
  render summaries sent to Design Lab and approved immutable package references sent to Social
  Distribution. Raw manuscript text, chunks, embeddings, private annotations and style exemplars
  never cross an edge.
- WebDev Signal has the same three narrow Instagram/Threads relationships: held GoVIRAL
  intelligence, bounded render summaries and approved immutable publish-package references. It has
  no content edge to Caught Up/DNESKAi, devShark or another venture. Website and house-promotion
  edges are outside the program; a later reconsideration requires a new owner decision and issue.

## Closed gates

This decision performs no provider call, publishing action, account connection, outreach,
purchase, monetization experiment or spend. Social Distribution connections, owner metrics,
provider credentials, routine publishing and every future monetization action remain closed until
their own recorded authority exists.
