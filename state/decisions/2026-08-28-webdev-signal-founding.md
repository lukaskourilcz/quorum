## Decision

BoardlessAI may build WebDev Signal as one Instagram-and-Threads-only web-development news venture
with two native editions, `cs` and `en`. `WebDev Signal` is retained as the working name; final
public use and exact social handles remain held for owner clearance. Instagram and Threads are the
entire public product for this program; there is no website stage or website backlog.

The desk owns one language-neutral evidence brief and independently written Czech and English
packages. It chooses zero or one material story per Prague day and records `NO_EDITION` instead of
filler. Official primary sources own factual authority. Secondary editorial sources can provide a
lead only. Both editions preserve the same facts, scope and uncertainty without requiring literal
translation or copied source wording.

## Boundaries

The capability map from #424 is authoritative. GoVIRAL may provide one held, expiring,
intelligence-only packet; it cannot create a candidate, establish a fact, force selection, provide
copy or start another collection run. WebDev Signal can send only a bounded accepted render summary
to Design Lab and an immutable approved package reference to Social Distribution. Those edges grant
no rendering, profile, connection, provider, routine-scope, queue or publishing authority.

Caught Up/DNESKAi, devShark, Personal Growth, Kvórum, Door Money, BOOKSOFHISTORY, Tehdejší svět,
FightAIQ/MMA Files, Contest Radar and unrelated ventures have no mandatory-core content edge in
either direction. Design Lab is the only renderer and Social Distribution the only future delivery
service. Website, public web surfaces and house promotion are outside this program. Any later
reconsideration requires a separate owner decision and a new issue and creates no current edge.

External accounts, OAuth, credentials, App Review, routine scopes and live activation are
owner-only. Automated engagement, ads, boosts, affiliate placement, sponsorship and monetization
are prohibited by this decision.

## Proposed envelope and cadence

Direct collection, extraction, selection and deterministic rendering target `$0` provider spend.
No model reservation occurs before deterministic selection. A selected day may later use one
bounded bilingual synthesis and at most one repair, proposed at a lower-of-authorities ceiling of
`$0.03` per day and `$0.75` per month. This proposal does not allocate or raise a budget. Missing
authority or headroom holds editorial output and cannot borrow from another venture.

The proposed `webdev-signal-daily` job shares the existing 05:00 Europe/Prague dispatcher firing as
an internal prerequisite before the Caught Up room. It creates no new cron, meeting or public
calendar row. Until later source and editorial activation, fixture mode costs `$0` and any live
invocation records an honest held/no-source/`NO_EDITION` result.

## Validation

The first 28 separately activated days are a baseline for source health and coverage, zero/one
selection consistency, duplicate/noise, corrections, bilingual parity and copy failures, cost,
render/queue/delivery reliability, valid official social outcomes and owner attention. After 60–90
days the owner records `CONTINUE`, `NARROW`, `PAUSE`, `RETIRE` or `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` using the
criteria in the founding audit. No follower or revenue target is authorized without evidence.

## Activation

Owner countersignature of `WEBDEV-SIGNAL-FOUNDING-001` confirms the working-name risk, source and
truth constitution, nested envelope and internal checkpoint. It still cannot activate a source,
model, account, renderer or publisher or waive any independent runtime gate.
