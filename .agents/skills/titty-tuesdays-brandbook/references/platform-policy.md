## Project doctrine

The venture takes a stricter route than the minimum platform text:

- Use product-first, type-first imagery with no generated people.
- Exclude nudity, sexual activity, suggestive poses, and isolated body parts.
- Let the brand name carry the joke. Do not add sexual supporting copy.
- Keep all social output in the draft queue while the kill switch is on.
- Do not plan or run paid ads until the owner approves accounts, spend, and a fresh policy review.

These are BoardlessAI controls. Do not present them as quotations from Meta.
