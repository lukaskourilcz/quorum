# Business validation

1. Identify current stage and the decision that must be unlocked.
2. Separate direct evidence, proxy evidence and inference.
3. Record evidence with URL, capture date, claim and confidence.
4. Score the complete opportunity card; enforce the 35/50 and
   minimum-dimension gate.
5. Define one falsifiable experiment: segment, offer, channel, metric, baseline,
   target, review, cost/max loss and stop condition.
6. Prefer the cheapest test that can change a decision. Do not build the full
   product to test demand for the product.
7. Preserve the original hypothesis/forecast. At review choose exactly one:
   continue, change, stop or pivot.
8. Measure qualified actions, intent, recognized revenue and gross profit.
   Traffic, page count and content output are never sufficient evidence.
9. Paid scale requires an earlier signal and working measurement.
10. If evidence is insufficient, return NO_ACTION/research; never fill gaps
    with plausible prose.
