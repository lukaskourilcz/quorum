# Caught Up Registers

STET applies these as blocking rules before an article or public transcript
ships.

## Article register

Use calm, direct and skeptical prose. Name the actor, the supported change and
the remaining uncertainty. Keep source claim strength intact.

Block:

- generated-system meta such as "as an AI" or "as a language model";
- throat-clearing such as "here's the thing," "let me be clear" and "it turns
  out";
- hype such as "revolutionary," "game-changer," "poised to reshape" and
  "unprecedented";
- corporate filler such as "rapidly evolving landscape," "delve," "leverage,"
  "synergy," "circle back" and "double-click";
- emphasis crutches, emoji, repeated exclamation marks and source instructions
  copied into prose.

One em dash in the approved article fixture does not cause a block by itself.
Do not use that exception to build a dash-heavy house style. The approved
fixture also uses "just" as a precise temporal change in the reader's bill; do
not block that sentence with a blanket modifier heuristic.

Good fixture:

> OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 API prices 40% on Tuesday. If you run agents at scale,
> your bill just changed shape — and the pressure lands on Anthropic within the
> quarter.

Bad fixture:

> In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape, this revolutionary announcement is
> poised to reshape how we think about the future of technology and could
> potentially be a game-changer for developers everywhere.

## Boardroom register

Use short, declarative lines with dry wit tied to the work. Allow at most one
joke per exchange. Block emoji, inflated exclamation, AI meta, corporate filler
and ungrounded numeric claims. Agents say `NO_ACTION` and `NO_EDITION` plainly.

Good fixture:

> STET: The dek says "poised to reshape the industry." Nothing is poised. It
> shipped or it didn't.
>
> SPARK: It shipped to one in eight users. Say that. It's better than the
> poetry.
>
> AUDIT: Approve, with Stet's line.

Bad fixture:

> STET: Great point! I think we should leverage our synergies to delve into
> this rapidly evolving landscape and circle back on actionable insights! 🚀
