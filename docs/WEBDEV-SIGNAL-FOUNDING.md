## Name and two-edition direction

`WebDev Signal` remains the working master brand, with `WebDev Signal CZ` and `WebDev Signal EN`
as edition labels. Logged-out exact-name searches on 2026-08-28 found no obvious same-name web
development publication or company. The nearby W3C WebDX `developer-signals` project and the
common use of “signal” for reactive APIs make the name descriptive, not cleared. Domain and public
handle availability was not stable enough to treat search results as a reservation.

Verdict: **keep as a working name; hold final public use** until the owner checks trademark risk,
the exact Instagram and Threads handles. This is product research, not legal clearance, and no
account was created.

One master brand keeps the source selection and factual record singular. Two named editions make
the locale promise visible without pretending one package is a translation of the other.

## Audiences

### Czech edition

The primary reader is a Czech- or Slovak-speaking working web developer, from an advancing junior
to a senior generalist, who wants a concise native-language answer to: what changed, whether it is
stable, who is affected and what to check next. The useful depth is one level below a headline and
one level above a migration guide. Instagram should carry a scannable visual explanation; Threads
should carry a compact source-linked note.

It excludes beginner course material, job listings, generic software news, unexplained English
jargon and invented Czech-market relevance. No audience size or demand is claimed.

### English edition

The primary reader is an international working web developer who already follows parts of the
ecosystem but wants a strict daily filter across browsers, languages, frameworks, runtimes,
tooling, platforms, standards and security. The job is to reduce monitoring time while preserving
versions, stability and scope. The expected depth is concise practitioner analysis, not a broad
news feed or tutorial.

It excludes link roundups, release-note dumps, generic AI coverage, influencer commentary and
rewritten vendor promotion. No market-size or follower forecast is asserted.

## Editorial constitution

In scope: browser and web-platform availability; HTML/CSS; JavaScript and TypeScript; frontend and
meta-frameworks; runtimes; package managers; build tooling; deployment platforms; security;
performance; accessibility; interoperability and standards; ecosystem governance or licensing
when it materially changes a developer's work.

Out of scope: rumors; jobs; events marketed without a material change; discounts, affiliate links
and paid placement; listicles and generic tutorials; minor maintenance releases with no declared
impact; generic AI news without a concrete web-development consequence; copied source wording;
engagement bait; commercial calls to action.

The desk selects zero or one canonical record per Europe/Prague day. `NO_EDITION` is a successful
decision when no record clears the materiality, authority, freshness and confidence gates.
Popularity and source volume cannot substitute for developer impact. Security, breaking and
deprecation claims require official affected and fixed versions or exact configuration scope.
Every edition links and attributes its fact authority. Secondary publications may discover a lead;
they cannot confirm it.

## Bilingual truth boundary

One language-neutral evidence brief owns the accepted claims, uncertainty, affected scope and
source links. Czech and English packages are independently written from that brief. They must carry
the same core claims and uncertainty, but need not share syntax, idiom or paragraph order.

Czech uses natural professional Czech, explains unavoidable English terms once and avoids literal
calques. English is compact international editorial English and does not assume US-only context.
Neither locale may introduce a version, benchmark, affected audience or recommendation absent from
the brief. A malformed locale is held without mutating the brief. One locale may proceed only when
the held reason is explicit and the proceeding locale is not factually broader. An unresolved
source conflict holds the affected claim and, when material, both editions.

## Cost and checkpoint proposal

Collection, extraction, canonicalization, scoring and deterministic Design Lab rendering have a
`$0` provider target. No model is reserved before a record wins selection. On a selected day, the
proposal permits one bilingual synthesis plus at most one bounded repair using the configured
default `gpt-5.6-luna` price verified in `orchestrator/src/llm/prices.ts`.

The proposed hard ceiling is `$0.03` per selected day and `$0.75` per calendar month. It is a lower
ceiling, never budget authority: the current treasury headroom and any later countersigned venture
allocation must also pass. Exhausted, missing or stale authority preserves the deterministic
selection and records editorial output as held; it never borrows from another venture or upgrades a
provider. No paid scheduler is needed.

The existing **05:00 Prague dispatcher firing** is the proposed checkpoint. WebDev Signal is an
internal prerequisite job, `webdev-signal-daily`, ahead of the Caught Up room rather than a new
meeting or cron. It uses the Prague date carried by the dispatcher, performs fixture-only `$0` work
until later activation, and finishes before Social Distribution's daily decision. The dispatcher
already emits distinct CET and CEST cron forms; #438 must prove the internal job adds neither a cron
nor a public calendar row and must reject writer/model/renderer/state-path collisions.

## Validation and stop conditions

The first 28 separately activated days establish a baseline for official-source availability and
coverage, empty/unchanged/malformed rates, duplicate/noise rate, zero/one decision consistency,
factual corrections, bilingual parity and copy-quality failures, cost per accepted edition,
render/queue/delivery reliability, official reach/saves/shares/referrals when available, and owner
attention. Fixture records and unavailable denominators are excluded rather than reported as zero.

At 60–90 days the owner records one result:

- `CONTINUE`: evidence, parity and delivery are reliable and the burden is acceptable;
- `NARROW`: value exists but source/topic/locale scope should shrink;
- `PAUSE`: a recoverable authority, reliability, cost or quality gate is failing;
- `RETIRE`: repeated low materiality or unacceptable risk/burden makes the desk unjustified;
- `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`: fewer than 28 valid live days or material metrics remain unavailable.

Any fabricated fact, unresolved material conflict, cross-venture leak, credential exposure,
duplicate remote publish, budget breach or action outside recorded scope immediately holds the
affected source, locale or connection and preserves evidence. There is no follower or revenue
target without a later evidence-backed decision.
