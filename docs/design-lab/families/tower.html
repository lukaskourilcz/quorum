Tower

Built for 9:16 first: a chapter bar under the platform chrome, a tall type stage in the middle, and a sticker line sitting above the bottom band where a thumb lands.

Do not use
A carousel where slide one must survive as a square thumbnail — Tower's stage is deliberately tall and empties out at 1:1.
Rhythm
cover (stage high, sticker accent) → stage high → stage low, chapter bar filled → stage high → outro. The type stage slides up and down inside the safe window while the bars stay put, so the frame is stable and the content moves.
