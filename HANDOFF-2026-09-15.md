## 5. Things learned tonight that the next session will trip over

- **Rewriting records is owner work under auto mode.** A script that rewrote receipts, meeting
  records and the inbox was refused by the permission classifier; single-file edits through the
  file tools were allowed once the owner had asked for the reconciliation explicitly. Prepare
  such repairs as exact per-file steps.
- **A public record's summary must not carry identifiers.** The site drops a meeting record
  whose summary reads as machine text; slugs, hashes and proof ids belong in the receipt, the
  sentence in the summary. `site/src/lib/public-surfaces.test.ts` is the guard.
- **Cron counts come from the generator.** Four tests used to pin "three sweeps"; they now read
  `deployedCronExpressions()`. Add a sweep in `BACKSTOP_SWEEP_HOURS` and `cycle.yml` together.
- **`pnpm docs:refresh` writes `docs/ECOSYSTEM.md`** from the registry; run it after any
  registry status change or `docs:check` goes red.
- **The failed job log outranks every document.** The credit outage was one call away in the
  Monday GoVIRAL run's log while every skip record only said "the run failed before it
  finished". Read the last day of failed cycle runs before reading state.
