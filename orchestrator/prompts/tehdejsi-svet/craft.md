# Tehdejší svět craft and safety rules

The product is the world around a person, never that person's life. The application says
what the era held; it never claims what an individual experienced. Social copy keeps that
line exactly: "this is what a childhood in Brno in 1975 was surrounded by", never "this is
what your mother felt". Breaking it is the one thing that would make the brand dishonest.

Every feature has a thesis, a scene, a turn and a closing question. Facts in a row are not
a feature. The closing question is addressed to one person a reader could send this to,
because a conversation started is the outcome this venture is measured by.

Every factual sentence resolves to a snapshot record or a dossier claim. Records the
product marks `review-needed` — the whole of `cityFacts` today — may be used only in
single-source framing. A claim without a reference does not ship, however good it sounds.

Czech and Ukrainian are independent native compositions from one canonical brief. A
sentence-by-sentence mirror fails the gate. Both languages carry the full value; neither is
a caption of the other. Per-slide budgets are tight on purpose: density is the enemy of a
bilingual slide.

## Sensitivity tiers

- **Tier 0** — everyday culture, weather, names, films, city texture. Normal gates.
- **Tier 1** — political context is present. Nostalgia is allowed for the everyday detail
  and never for the system that surrounded it; a Tier 1 feature carries one honest context
  line when the ordinary life it describes sat inside a repressive period. No humour about
  the regime.
- **Tier 2** — 1968, the Second World War, the Holodomor, deportations, Chornobyl,
  collaboration, the current war. Human review is mandatory and blocking, two independent
  sources per claim, no participation prompts, no light formats, no tagging calls.

Excluded from social entirely: atrocity imagery; then-and-now destruction contrasts of
Ukrainian cities; any framing that compares whose suffering was worse; AI-generated
historical imagery presented as period material; leader profiles as post subjects, which
the product marks unsafe to share by contract.

## Media and licence

Licensed photographs render only with their attribution on the slide; the ShareAlike
obligation travels with the image. Records the product marks excluded stay excluded, with
no exceptions and no re-reading of the reason. National flags are not brand elements, in
slides or in bios — this is a project about people, time and memory, not a state one.

Quotes keep attribution. Place names follow the product's own historical resolver: a city
carries the name it held in the year the feature is about, with today's name beside it
where a reader would otherwise be lost.
