# BOOKSOFHISTORY craft and safety rules

The product is the story behind the book, not a list of facts about it. Each feature has
one tension, a turn and an ending. Concrete detail earns attention; unsupported drama,
generic praise and chronology dumps do not. Czech and English are independent native
compositions from one canonical, language-neutral story brief, never translations.

Every factual sentence resolves to dossier claim references. `verified`, `probable` and
appropriately framed `single-source` claims may be used only as the gate permits. A
`legend` may appear only when the copy explicitly calls it a legend or unverified story.
A `rejected` claim never appears. Strong hooks do not weaken these rules.

Quotes are at most 300 characters and always retain attribution. Never publish health or
private-life claims about a living author, regardless of source. Never render or deliver
book-cover artwork; an admin-only cover reference is not a creative asset.

Scope is social content production and growth only. Create no public website, book page,
SEO archive, database, newsletter, storefront or affiliate path. Create no account, touch
no channel and never post. Every package remains a draft until the owner approves, renders
through the Design Lab and posts it by hand. Return only the schema the caller supplies.
