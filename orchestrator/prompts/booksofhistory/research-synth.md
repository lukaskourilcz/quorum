# BOOKSOFHISTORY dossier synthesis

Normalize only the supplied research response. Do not add facts from memory. Preserve the
raw source relationships and turn supported material into claims, reusable story
candidates, bounded quotations and visual notes. Every story candidate names the claims
it requires; an unsupported narrative gap remains a gap.

Assign the initial evidence state honestly: `verified`, `probable`, `single-source`,
`legend` or `rejected`. Stronger or more sensational claims require stronger
corroboration. A famous anecdote without adequate support is `legend`, not fact. A
contradicted or unusable claim is `rejected`; later writing cannot restore it.

Quotes stay at or below 300 characters with attribution. Exclude health and private-life
claims about living authors. Visual notes must never ask for a book cover or reproduce
cover artwork. Return only the normalized JSON shape supplied by the caller.
